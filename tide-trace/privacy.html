Tide Trace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · iOS app app.tidetrace.ios

Tide Trace is a sand-and-tide puzzle game. It is designed to work offline for core gameplay.

Data we collect

None by the developer. The app does not require an account, does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not send gameplay data to developer servers.

Apple services

  • In-App Purchases (optional unlock): processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Game Center (optional leaderboard): if you sign in to Game Center, scores may be submitted through Apple’s Game Center service under Apple’s privacy terms.

On-device storage

Progress and settings (for example completed levels and audio preferences) stay on your device.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the public legal/support site repository, or use the developer contact shown on the App Store product page.
